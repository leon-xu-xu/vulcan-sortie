Vulcan Sortie - Support

iOS and Android - published by Arcade Works. See also the Privacy Policy.

Vulcan Sortie is a single-player vertical arcade shooter: one jet against the Hive, seven stages, seven bosses. There is no account, no login and nothing to buy. This page answers the common questions; if yours is not here, contact us at the bottom.

1. How to play

ActionTouch
MoveDrag anywhere on the screen. The jet follows your finger; you do not have to touch the jet itself.
FireAutomatic while you are touching the screen.
BombTap the plate in the bottom-left corner. Clears bullets and damages everything on screen. Limited stock; pick up B icons for more.
OverdriveTap the plate in the bottom-right corner once its gauge is full. A short burst of maximum fire power.
PauseTap the pause button at the top centre of the screen. Sending the app to the background pauses as well.

Pickups

Coloured power chips raise your fire power one step and switch the main weapon to the matching type: red Vulcan (spread), blue Laser (piercing), orange F Fireball (spinning orbs). B adds a bomb, medals and gems add score, and the rare extend gives you an extra ship.

Ships, credits and continues

You start with three ships on one credit. When the last ship is lost a 30-second countdown offers a continue. If a rewarded ad is available, WATCH AD lets you continue after the ad; otherwise CONTINUE is free. Declining ends the run and records your score.

Modes

Arcade is the full campaign and the only mode that records scores. Practice lets you start from any stage you have reached in Arcade. Boss Rush chains the bosses back to back and unlocks once you have reached stage 2.

2. Frequently asked questions

I only see CONTINUE, never WATCH AD. Where is the ad?

The offer appears only when an ad has finished loading. It is normal for the first run after install, without a network connection, or in regions where no ad is available. On iOS 27 and newer the game currently does not load ads at all, so the continue is always free there; this is deliberate until the ad provider supports the new system version.

I did not see the ADS AND PRIVACY card or the tracking prompt.

Both appear only when ads are active on your device (see the previous answer). If they are, the card is shown once at first launch and can be reopened any time under OPTIONS > ADS. On iOS the system tracking prompt appears only after you choose ALLOW; change it later in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.

The ad played but I did not get my continue.

The continue is granted when the network reports the ad as completed. If the ad was closed early, the connection dropped mid-video, or the app was sent to the background, the reward can be lost. Watching to the end with a stable connection fixes it in almost every case. Nothing is charged either way.

How do I turn off personalised ads?

OPTIONS > ADS > LIMIT ADS. You still get the continue offer with non-personalised ads. Details are in the Privacy Policy.

The game is too hard. Is there an easier mode?

Vulcan Sortie ships with a single difficulty tuned for short arcade runs. Tips: keep moving, the jet has a small hitbox so bullets that look close usually miss; save bombs for boss patterns you cannot read yet; Practice lets you replay any stage you have reached.

Performance, stutter or heat

The game runs at your display's native refresh rate while playing. It steps down to 60 frames per second on its own when the phone cannot hold a higher rate cleanly, when the chip is hot, or when the battery is under 20% and discharging; that is deliberate pacing, not a fault. Closing other apps and removing a thick case helps a warm phone.

Sound or haptics are missing

Check the MUSIC, SFX and HAPTICS rows under OPTIONS, and the phone's silent switch and system vibration setting. PHONE MUSIC set to YIELD mutes the game's music while another app is playing audio. Haptics need a phone with a haptic engine; iPad has none.

My progress or high scores disappeared

Scores, medals and options are stored on the device only (no cloud save, no account). Uninstalling the game or clearing its data removes them, and they cannot be restored by us. Reinstalling starts fresh.

Can I play with a controller?

Touch is the supported input on phones and tablets. Controller support is on the wish list.

3. Bug reports

Please include: device model, system version, the game version shown on its App Store or Google Play page, what you were doing, and a screenshot or screen recording if you have one. Crash reports shared through the App Store or Google Play reach us as well.

4. Refunds and purchases

Vulcan Sortie is free and has no in-app purchases, so there is nothing to refund. If you see a charge you do not recognise, contact Apple or Google, as the payment was not made to us.
