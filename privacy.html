Vulcan Sortie - Privacy Policy

Effective 20 September 2026 - applies to the iOS and Android versions of Vulcan Sortie ("the game") published by Arcade Works.

Vulcan Sortie is a single-player arcade shooter. It has no account, no login, no chat, no in-app purchases and no analytics of its own. The only data processing in the game comes from the advertising network that serves the optional "watch an ad to continue" offer after you lose your last ship. This page explains what that involves and the choices you have.

1. Data the game itself stores

Your scores, medals, options (volume, haptics, controls) and your advertising choice are saved on your device only, in the game's own storage. They never leave the device and are deleted when you uninstall the game.

2. Advertising (Unity LevelPlay / ironSource)

Rewarded video ads are served through Unity LevelPlay (ironSource, a Unity company) with Unity Ads as the mediated network. An ad is requested only when you have lost all your ships and a continue is offered; the SDK may pre-load the next ad in the background so it is ready at that moment.

To serve, deliver and measure ads, these partners may collect:

DataPurpose
Advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS - only if you allow tracking; Google Advertising ID on Android), vendor identifier (IDFV)Ad frequency capping, fraud prevention and, where you have consented, personalised ads and attribution

These partners act as independent controllers of that data. Their policies: Unity Privacy Policy and Unity player privacy policy (covers Unity Ads and LevelPlay / ironSource).

3. Your choices

In the game

On first launch the ADS AND PRIVACY card asks whether you allow personalised ads. ALLOW lets the network use your advertising identifier; LIMIT ADS tells the network you do not consent (GDPR) and to not sell or share your data (CCPA/CPRA) - you still get the continue offer, with non-personalised ads. You can change this at any time under OPTIONS > ADS.

On iOS

Personalised ads additionally require your permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt, which the game shows after you choose ALLOW. You can change it later in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. The game works fully if you decline.

On Android

You can reset or delete your Google Advertising ID under Settings > Google > Ads.

4. Children

Vulcan Sortie is not directed at children under 13 and is rated for ages 9 and up for cartoon violence. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children; the advertising network is configured for a general audience.

5. Data retention and transfers

The game stores nothing about you on our servers - we operate none. Advertising partners retain the data described above according to their own policies and may process it in the United States and other countries under appropriate safeguards (for EU/UK users, standard contractual clauses).

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live (EU/EEA, UK, California and other US states, Brazil, and others) you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict processing of your personal data, to object to profiling, and to opt out of the sale or sharing of your data. Because we hold no data ourselves, requests about advertising data should be directed to Unity at the links above; you can also exercise the opt-outs described in section 3 directly. For anything else, contact us below.
